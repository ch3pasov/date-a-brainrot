# Source release — 2 November 2026

The live Roblox game was opened to the public without changing its checked
version 29 gameplay. Roblox's content-maturity and regional restrictions apply;
the Creator Dashboard reports availability for eligible players aged 18+.

Included source implements swipes with immediate or delayed probabilistic
matches, scripted phone messages, a gesture video call, three date locations,
character variants, persistent best scores, and a shared-selfie album.

Version 29 had 285 successful offline checks, both Roblox build formats, targeted
compact story layouts, a complete new chat/call/park path, and successful ordinary
rejoin persistence. Public-source verification uses an empty art container; it
does not prove that the public build reproduces the live game's character art.

Unresolved limitations: independent friend-account entry was not confirmed;
physical-device/FPS and audio QA remain incomplete; the naturally delivered
late-match letter UI was not observed in the final runtime; the owner has not
approved the final visual direction. These are retained limitations, not claims
of completed testing. No real-world dating between players is implemented.
