# Third-party materials

## Not distributed

The private development checkout used a Roblox Creator Store pack listed as
**FaIertyup**, asset **80086582263978**:
[original listing](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/80086582263978).
Its models, meshes, textures, derived character exports, animation data tied to
those rigs, portraits, compiled game files, and research archives are omitted from
this public repository. An empty, newly authored Models/Clips folder structure
replaces the private model collection. The original private Git history, tags,
screenshots, logs, and packages are not published here.

The listing was acquired through Roblox's ordinary inventory flow. That is not
evidence that the uploader owns every underlying character, texture, or brand,
and this project does not assert that such rights have been cleared. In
particular, no permission to redistribute the pack outside Roblox is asserted.

Relevant platform sources, reviewed on 2 November 2026:

- [Creator Store Terms](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/21308223046932-Creator-Store-Terms)
- [Creator Store documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store)

## Source and platform dependencies

The Luau gameplay, UI, procedural scenery, test harnesses, and Python development
tools in this snapshot were authored for this project. Character names in the
catalog identify existing meme characters; they do not transfer rights in those
characters. Roblox APIs, fonts, and the built-in `rbxasset://sounds/volume_slider.ogg`
are referenced through the Roblox runtime; their underlying files are not bundled.
Player avatar appearance is fetched by Roblox at runtime; no player's outfit,
profile, or saved game data is included in this source release.

Rokit, Rojo, Lune, and the GitHub Actions used for verification are external
dependencies, referenced rather than vendored. They remain under their respective
licenses. The public workflow has read-only repository permissions and no Roblox
deployment job or publishing credential.

## Adding art

Acquire art independently from a source that authorizes your intended use. Check
both the platform license and underlying third-party rights. Credit the creator
and meet any stated conditions. Do not commit that art, modified copies, preview
images, or compiled places to this repository without a separate redistribution
permission that covers it.
